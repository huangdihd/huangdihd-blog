# Rain scene audio

- `rain.ogg`: “Rain (1)” by ezwa / PDSounds, public domain. Original recording, unchanged.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_(1).ogg
- `drips.ogg`: “Water drops dripping” by ZooFari, CC BY-SA 3.0. Original recording, unchanged.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Water_drops_dripping.ogg
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
  This records a faucet dripping into a bowl of water, used as a substitute for windowsill drips, not a recording of the author's room.

Playback loops and adjusts volume locally. No synthesized fan noise, noise generator, or oscillator is mixed into these recordings.
